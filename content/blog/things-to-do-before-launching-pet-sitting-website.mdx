As a pet sitter or dog walker, a [professional website](https://www.petsfriendz.com/blog/everything-to-include-on-your-pet-sitting-website) is one of the most effective tools to promote your services. Not only does it help you build a memorable, trustworthy brand, but it can help you tap into valuable SEO traffic that brings customers to you. In fact, some say it's never too early to launch your site and start building your online presence.

But especially for sitters just starting out, there are a few steps you can take to maximize growth before investing time and money into your digital marketing plan.

## 1. Gain Real Pet Care Experience

While pet care may sound like a fun hobby, it's not always as easy as it looks. Before you launch a website, or any kind of marketing for your pet care services, you'll want to gain some hands-on experience caring for animals. Not only will this help you build a solid resume to promote to potential clients, but it’ll also ensure that you enjoy the day-to-day realities of the job before you dive into it.

Start by volunteering at local animal shelters or offering to pet sit for friends and family. The more experience you can gain with different animals, the better prepared you’ll be for handling different pet personalities on the job. If you can develop specialized skills like administering medication or handling large dogs, you’ll be able to list these on your website to bring in a wider range of clients.

## 2. Build Up References and Collect Testimonials

One of the most powerful elements of any service-based website is social proof, and pet sitting is no exception. When potential clients visit your site, they want to see evidence that you're trustworthy, reliable, and good at what you do. After all, anyone who has a pet of their own knows that it takes a lot to trust them with somebody else.

Before launching your website, focus on building a portfolio of happy clients. As you sit for friends, family, and early clients, ask them for:

* Written testimonials or reviews  
* Photos of you with their pets  
* Permission to use them as references if needed

Having 5-10 strong testimonials ready to feature on your website from day one makes a major difference in how your business will appear to potential clients.

## 3. Get Proper Insurance Coverage

One of the biggest mistakes beginner pet sitters and dog walkers can make is neglecting to get their own insurance. Even when sitting for friends and family, pet sitting insurance is critical to protecting everyone involved. Accidents happen, and when they do, the financial and legal consequences can be devastating without proper coverage.

Many new pet sitters assume that working through [apps like Rover or Wag\!](https://www.petsfriendz.com/blog/breaking-down-top-pet-sitting-apps) provides enough protection, but relying solely on platform coverage leaves gaps in your protection. Having your own insurance policy ensures you're covered regardless of how you find clients, and it protects both your business and your personal assets from liability claims.

When you do launch your website, being able to display that you're fully insured is also a valuable part of your pitch. It demonstrates professionalism and gives potential clients peace of mind that their pets and property are protected under your care.
