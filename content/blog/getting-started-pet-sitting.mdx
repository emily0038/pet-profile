## Welcome to Pet Sitting

Starting a pet sitting business can be an incredibly rewarding venture. Whether you're looking to turn your love for animals into a full-time career or earn extra income on the side, this guide will help you get started.

<Callout>
**Pro Tip:** Building trust with pet owners is the foundation of a successful pet sitting business. Always be transparent about your experience and certifications.
</Callout>

## Essential Steps to Launch Your Business

- Get certified in pet first aid and CPR
- Create your professional profile on Pets Friendz
- Set competitive rates for your area
- Build a portfolio with photos and testimonials
- Establish clear policies for cancellations and emergencies

## Setting Your Rates

Pricing your services appropriately is crucial. Research what other pet sitters in your area charge, and consider factors like:

1. Your experience level and certifications
2. The type of service (overnight stays vs. drop-in visits)
3. Number of pets and their specific needs
4. Additional services like administering medication

<Callout>
**Success Story:** Many pet sitters start with competitive introductory rates and gradually increase their prices as they build a strong reputation and client base.
</Callout>
